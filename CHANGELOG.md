# Changelog

## v0.1.0

First release.

- `scan`: find runs that executed a compromised action in its exposure window, across repositories, organizations and users; follow composite actions and reusable workflows; confirm or clear runs from their logs; find dumped secret names; rank the secrets to rotate.
- `usage`: list where an action is used today, directly or nested, with its pin type.
- `incidents`: built-in catalogue of tj-actions (2025), reviewdog (2025), xygeni (2026) and trivy-action (2026).
- Text, Markdown and JSON output; CI exit codes.
